Nernst Equation for Half-Cell Potential

Connects electrochemical cell potential to reactant/product quotient concentrations at variable temperatures.

E = E° - (2.303 RT / nF) log₁₀ Q
At T = 298 K: E = E° - (0.0591 / n) log₁₀ Q
history_edu Tested in 7 JEE Main papers & 5 CBSE Board Exams (Last 6 Years).
1 Thermodynamic link: ΔG = ΔG° + RT ln Q where Q is reaction quotient.
2 Electrical relation: Substitute ΔG = -nFE and ΔG° = -nFE°. Divide both sides by -nF to yield the final form.

Faraday's First & Second Laws of Electrolysis

1st Law (Mass Deposited) m = Z · I · t

Mass m liberated is directly proportional to passed charge Q (Z = M / (n · 96500)).

2nd Law (Equivalent Series) w₁ / w₂ = E₁ / E₂

When the identical quantity of charge traverses several electrolytes, masses are proportional to their chemical equivalents.

Kohlrausch Law of Independent Migration

At infinite dilution, each ion makes a definite contribution toward equivalent conductance irrespective of partner co-ion.

Λ°m = ν₊ λ°₊ + ν₋ λ°₋
Crucial application: Calculating limiting molar conductivity for weak electrolytes like CH₃COOH.

Common JEE Traps & Conventions

priority_high Sign Convention Alert: IUPAC requires all electrode potentials written as Standard Reduction Potentials (SRP). If given E°(Zn/Zn²⁺) = +0.76 V, flip it to E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = -0.76 V before substituting in E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode!
lightbulb SHE Standard: Standard Hydrogen Electrode potential is assumed exactly 0.00 V at all temperatures by international definition, not experiment.
JEE Main 2023 Shift 2 • 4 Marks 90 sec timed

For the cell reaction 2Fe³⁺(aq) + 2I⁻(aq) → 2Fe²⁺(aq) + I₂(s), the value of E°cell = 0.236 V at 298 K. The standard Gibbs energy (ΔrG°) is:

Correct Value: -45.55 kJ/mol Formula: -nFE°

1. Determine electrons exchanged: n = 2.
2. Apply relation: ΔrG° = -n F E°cell
3. Calculation: -(2) × 96500 C × 0.236 V = -45548 J/mol ≈ -45.55 kJ/mol.
