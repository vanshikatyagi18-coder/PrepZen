DNA Replication Fork Mechanism

4 Critical Steps
1

Initiation & Unwinding

Helicase

DNA Helicase breaks hydrogen bonds at the replication origin (oriC), exposing single strands and forming the classic Y-shaped replication fork.

2

Stabilization & Tension Relief

SSB & Topo

SSB proteins keep separated template strands apart. Topoisomerase (Gyrase) cleaves and reconnects DNA to release supercoiling torsional strain upstream.

3

Asymmetric Synthesis (5' → 3')

DNA Pol III

Leading strand is synthesized continuously. The lagging strand is synthesized discontinuously into transient 1000-2000 bp Okazaki fragments.

4

Primer Excision & Ligation

DNA Ligase

RNA primers are excised and replaced by DNA Pol I. DNA Ligase stitches adjacent Okazaki fragments, producing two semiconservative duplexes.

⭐ NCERT MUST-KNOW (Repeated 6x NEET)

Okazaki fragments are linked explicitly by DNA Ligase via phosphodiester bond creation using ATP/NAD+ hydrolysis. DNA Polymerase itself CANNOT join free 3'-OH and 5'-phosphate nicks.
